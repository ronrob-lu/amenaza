# Amenaza — 2026 Luanti Game Jam Entry

This repository is an official entry for the **2026 Luanti Game Jam**, built for **Luanti 5.18.0** (compatible with Luanti 5.8+).

## Concept Summary

**Amenaza** is an atmospheric, inventory-free survival thriller set inside a sprawling 5,000 × 5,000 neoclassical European city on the brink of a total power grid collapse.

- **Phase 1 — Unstable Dawn (7-Minute Countdown):** You spawn in the Grand Central Plaza facing a restless protest crowd gathered beneath flickering street lanterns and surging building facades. A rogue AI mainframe is draining the city's power grid. Talk to distinctive protesters in the crowd to learn what is happening and get live compass bearings, or trace the heavy yellow-striped power conduits running through the boulevards to locate the fortified AI Data Center.
- **Phase 2 — Total Blackout & Riot:** If the 7-minute countdown expires before you reach the Mainframe Core, the entire city plunges into darkness. Streetlights and windows extinguish, and the panicked crowd turns violent—attacking both each other and you.
- **Two Endings:**
  - **Victory:** Breach the AI Data Center and interact with the **Mainframe Core** to shut down the rogue AI, instantly calming the city, clearing the rioters, and restoring steady daylight and power.
  - **Game Over (Spectator Mode):** If you fall in the darkness, you seamlessly transition into a free-flying ghost spectator able to observe the darkened city without losing the atmosphere.

## Development Notes

- **Standalone Architecture:** Built from scratch on the `singlenode` mapgen with a deterministic VoxelManip urban generator (`amenaza_mapgen`), custom architectural and power-grid instability nodes (`amenaza_nodes`), crowd/dialogue/riot AI (`amenaza_npcs`), core state machine and atmosphere controller (`amenaza_core`), and bundled `hud_compass` for spatial navigation.
- **Zero Inventory / Diegetic Design:** Hotbar, crafting, and digging are intentionally disabled so the player navigates purely through environmental storytelling—flickering grid waves, power cables, crowd dialogue, and the HUD compass.
- **Bilingual Support:** Full English and Spanish localization (`auto`, `/idioma`, `/lang`) across all HUD banners, dialogues, node labels, and commands.
- **Jam Compliance:**
  - Self-contained under `mods/` with zero external dependencies.
  - Starts immediately in the cohesive game experience upon creating a new world.
